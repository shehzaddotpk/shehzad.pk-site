02 — HOW I WORK

Five principles.

The same sequence, whatever the size of the project or the technology involved.

  1. 01

    Understand the Requirement

    Start with the business objective. Clarify who the work is for, what success looks like, what is out of scope and what constraints apply — before anything is estimated.

  2. 02

    Plan the Delivery

    Turn the requirement into scope, priorities, milestones and owners. Make the plan visible so everyone knows what is happening next and why.

  3. 03

    Coordinate the Team

    Align designers, developers and stakeholders around the same picture. Remove blockers, make decisions quickly and keep communication direct.

  4. 04

    Execute & Monitor

    Track progress against the plan, surface risks early, and protect quality as the work moves from build to testing to launch.

  5. 05

    Improve & Optimize

    Review what worked and what didn’t. Refine the process so the next project starts from a better baseline.
